About Us

CobVar is a comprehensive resource of genetic variants associated with 6 different Vitamin B12 Database related traits including circulating Serum/Plasma Vitamin B12 levels, Holotranscobalamin, Holohaptocorrin, Methylmalonic acid (MMA), and Homocysteine, manually curated from studies conducted across multiple ethnicities and geographies.
